4. Statistical Exploration & Findings

  • Non-Linear Diffusion: The coordinates jump across the grid field \(\mathbb{Z}_{17}\) in a pseudo-random pattern, illustrating why the ECDLP problem remains intractable.
  • Avalanche Effect: Small perturbations in the private scalar key \(k\) completely shift the resulting elliptic curve coordinate.
