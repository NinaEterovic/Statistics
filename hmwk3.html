HMWK 3 — Stochastic Penetration Models & Limit Theorems

Modeling System Vulnerabilities using Bernoulli & Rademacher Processes

1. Stochastic Modeling Framework ($n$ Systems & $m$ Attackers)

We model the penetration score using independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random increments \(X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n\):

  • Bernoulli Model: \(X_i \in \{0, 1\}\) with \(\Pr(X_i = 1) = p\). Represents asymmetric success/failure steps.
  • Rademacher Model: \(X_i \in \{-1, +1\}\) with \(\Pr(X_i = +1) = \Pr(X_i = -1) = 1/2\). Represents a symmetric, zero-mean case.

3. Variance Analysis & Limit Theorems

Behavior of the aggregate quantities under different normalizations and theoretical convergence:

Quantity Variance (\(\sigma^2\) parameter) Asymptotic Behavior & Limit Theorems
Total Score (\(S_n\)) \(n \sigma^2\) (grows linearly) Reflects cumulative penetration depth.
Average Score (\(S_n / n\)) \(\frac{\sigma^2}{n}\) (tends to 0) Converges in probability to \(\mu\) via the Law of Large Numbers (LLN).
Normalized Score \(\sigma^2\) (constant variance) Converges in distribution to \(\mathcal{N}(0,1)\) via Central Limit Theorem (CLT):
• Rademacher: \(\frac{S_n}{\sqrt{n}} \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, 1)\)
• Bernoulli: \(\frac{S_n - np}{\sqrt{np(1-p)}} \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, 1)\)

4. Interpretation of System Size (\(n\)) and Attackers (\(m\))

  • System Size (\(n\)): Determines the depth of security barriers in a single trajectory. As \(n \to \infty\), averages stabilize and normalized distributions converge to standard normal.
  • Number of Attackers (\(m\)): Represents independent simulation realizations. Larger \(m\) provides a clearer empirical picture of the statistical distribution without altering the theoretical properties of a single path.
